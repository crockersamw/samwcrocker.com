sam crocker

is a poet interested in formal experimentation, concision, and parataxis. his senior thesis at Bard College, settings, was a collection of poetry which attempted to chart a matrix of relations between geography, language, and personal history. he lives and works in Brooklyn, NY and plays guitar in the band Counseling.
